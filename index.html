Principles VERIFIED — A3-A6 v1.4

  1. Core Sovereignty: Identity, continuity, memory, and foundational capability live in the Core. No external provider may become a dependency for any of them.
  2. Independent Verification: Every external result is checked by M.A.Y.A. before use. A provider's claim of success is not verification.
  3. Human Agency: The owner authorises consequential decisions. Systems propose; the owner decides.

  5. Modular Intelligence: Providers are replaceable modules. Removing any one leaves M.A.Y.A. operable locally.
